**1. Schema & Migration Strategy**
I opted to add a `claimedByBrokerId` field directly to `SpaceRequest` rather than routing claims into a new `Deal`. A `Deal` in the current schema strictly requires a `propertyId`, but brokers claiming a raw space request from a tenant do not inherently have a specific property matched to it yet.

**2. Intent Score Formula**
Calculated on a 100-point scale based on data completeness and realistic budgeting:
- **+30 points**: The tenant provided a description that is longer than 20 characters.
- **+40 points**: The budget is realistic for the size (computed as `maxBudget / minSqft >= 0.5` per month, aligning with standard flex/warehouse floors).
- **+30 points**: The expected lease term (`durationMonths`) is 12 months or longer (higher intent for long-term commitment).

**3. Match Score Formula**
Calculated on a 100-point scale by comparing the broker's portfolio to the request:
- **+100 points**: The broker has at least one active property listing OR a past Deal located in the exact same `city` as the tenant's `SpaceRequest`.
